# M6.1 HCP-YA Transversal Review Under V3

**Status:** prospective review written after M6.1

This review interprets the completed M6.1 result under
`hrp_stack_variable_registry_v3`. V3 was formulated after M6.1 and does not
change the frozen M6.1 protocol, model set, split, metrics, numerical analysis
or report.

## Scope

M6.1 is a Capability / State analysis. It tested whether frozen K/C_candidate/V
coordinates predicted held-out ListSort, PMAT accuracy and secondary CardSort
performance in the official HCP 100 unrelated subjects.

It did not estimate:

```text
L_WM
L_attention
L_predictive
L_reasoning
OP_binding
OP_relational
OP_predictive
A_evidence
T_commit
PC_calibration
```

## C Interpretation

The M6.1 C_candidate result is narrow. Flanker-derived C predicted independent
CardSort performance beyond K, but did not demonstrate broad C transport to
ListSort or PMAT.

Under V3 this is compatible with C being a control-specific capability
candidate whose deployment may depend on task control demand. It is not a
confirmed C_control factor and does not establish a general strategic system.

## V Interpretation

The V_candidate result should remain a vigilance/readiness candidate result.
V may be state/session sensitive and is not required to behave like stable K.
M6.1 does not validate a vigilance mechanism.

## Representational Interpretation

M6.1 residual variation must remain descriptive. It is not `L_WM`, W, WM
capacity, a layer-specific factor, a binding operator, a relational operator or
a bottleneck.

M6.2 should therefore ask the ontology-neutral question:

```text
After frozen K/C/V influences are accounted for, do two independent WM tasks
share reliable held-out individual-difference variance?
```

If positive, the allowed label is `WM_shared_specific_candidate` or shared
within-WM variance beyond K/C/V. The result would not decide whether the
specific variance is organised by H_LAYER, H_OPERATOR or H_HYBRID.

## Forward Gate

No M6.2 model should be fit until a valid independently reconstructed HCP
2-back behavioural measure passes support for the same official 100 unrelated
subjects. `WM_Task_2bk_Acc` remains ineligible while its registered concern is
unresolved.
